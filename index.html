Frequently asked questions

What counts as a peak hour?

Peak hours are 07:00-10:00 and 16:00-19:00. These are reserved for our Peak rate to guarantee your 1-hour slot when the city is busiest.

How does the Volume discount work?

If you ship more than 100 drops a month and schedule them during our off-peak hours (10:00-16:00), you qualify for our €4.50 Volume rate.

How long is a delivery slot?

Every delivery is made within a precise 1-hour slot chosen by you.

How much CO2e does a bike drop emit?

0.03 kg CO2e per drop by bike, compared with 0.60 kg by van.
